The house

A workshop before it was a brand.

Three generations of carpenters, polishers and upholsterers in Jodhpur, making for other people's names since 1962.

Charpai brought that making under one roof in 2019, with its own drawing office and its own standard of finish, so a piece made in Jodhpur can hold its own in any room.

Today we cover the whole interior, from the first measurement to the last cushion.

Process

A room is measured before it is designed.

i.

Survey

Every wall measured and photographed, with the details that decide a fit.

ii.

Drawing

Your references become a dimensioned drawing, with fabric and finish called out.

iii.

Making

Carpentry, polish and upholstery on our own floor, to the drawing.

iv.

Finish

Checked in daylight against the drawing before anything leaves.

v.

Install

Fitted by the team that made it, and handed over complete.
